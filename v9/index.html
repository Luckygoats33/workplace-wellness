Choose your pace

Four levels of participation

No one is forced into anything. Every attendee chooses how deeply to engage — from quiet observation to full immersion.

  • Observe

    Watch and absorb from a comfortable distance. Perfect for skeptics or those who prefer to ease in gradually.

  • Participate

    Join the guided activities at your own intensity. Follow along, modify as needed — no pressure.

  • Engage

    Actively take part in discussions, ask questions, and explore techniques more deeply with the facilitator.

  • Immerse

    Go all in. Extended sessions, one-on-one guidance, and the full depth of each modality.

The program

Structured for balance

Choose a half-day introduction or a full-day immersion. Both formats follow a natural arc from grounding to integration.

Half day

The introduction

A focused 3–4 hour session ideal for team off-sites, lunch-and-learns, or wellness days. Covers breathwork, sound, and movement with a closing integration circle.

Full day

The immersion

A complete 6–8 hour journey with morning activation, midday sound healing, afternoon movement, and evening wind-down. Includes breaks, meals, and personal reflection time.

Common questions

Frequently asked

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend 4–6 weeks for scheduling flexibility, but we've accommodated events with as little as 2 weeks' notice depending on facilitator availability.

What space and equipment do we need to provide?

A quiet room large enough for your group to sit or lie down comfortably. We bring all equipment — bowls, mats, speakers, and props. You provide the space and any A/V if presentations are included.

Can we customize which activities are included?

Absolutely. Every program is tailored. You choose which modalities to include, the pace, and even how the day is structured. We'll guide you based on your goals and group size.

What about attendees who are skeptical or uncomfortable?

That's exactly why we built four participation levels. No one is forced into anything. Skeptics can observe, and most end up participating once they see the experience is low-pressure and genuinely restorative.

Do you travel to our location?

Yes — we come to you. On-site delivery is our standard model. We've facilitated in offices, conference venues, retreat centers, and outdoor spaces. Travel logistics are included in our proposal.

What does pricing look like?

Pricing depends on group size, program length (half-day vs. full-day), and any optional enhancements. We provide a transparent quote after our initial conversation — no surprises.

Do I need any experience with yoga or meditation?

None at all. Every session is designed for complete beginners. Our facilitators guide you through every step, and you go at your own pace.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing you can move in. Business casual works for most sessions. If movement or yoga is included, stretchy or loose-fitting clothes are ideal.

What if I don't want to participate in everything?

That's completely fine. You choose your own level of participation — observe, join partially, or go all in. There's no judgment and no pressure.

Will this be weird or too "out there"?

We get it. These sessions are professional, grounded, and evidence-based. Think less incense-and-crystals, more structured relaxation techniques used by athletes and executives worldwide.

I have a physical limitation — can I still participate?

Yes. All activities have seated or modified versions. Let us know about any accommodations in advance and we'll ensure you're comfortable throughout.
