    Questions from event planners

    What do we need to provide?

    An open floor area for standing and seated movement, standard chairs, basic AV for music, a water station, and attendee communication before the event. Facilitators coordinate any specific requirements in advance.

    What's included in the price?

    Facilitator time, preparation, pre-event coordination, approved materials, and follow-up resources. Pricing varies by program, duration, and number of facilitators. We'll provide a transparent quote after learning about your event.

    Can we combine multiple specialists?

    Yes. SWG coordinates multi-expert events based on facilitator availability. Share your interests and we'll recommend a suitable combination.

    What about multi-day or repeat programs?

    Extended formats are available for leadership retreats and ongoing wellness partnerships. Discuss your goals during planning.

    Do you handle large groups?

    Yes. Programs scale from 20 to 500+ attendees. Larger groups may use breakout sessions, multiple facilitators, or staggered scheduling — we'll design the format during planning.

    Questions from attendees

    What if I can't do certain movements?

    Every activity offers standing, seated, and observing options. You choose your level throughout the session. Facilitators introduce all options at the start — no one is singled out.

    Do I need to share personal information?

    No. Group discussions are optional, and no one is required to share anything personal. Participation is always your choice.

    Is this a medical program?

    No. This is an educational wellness experience, not a medical or therapeutic program. Activities are guided movement, breathing education, and practical wellness concepts. Facilitators are not providing medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have specific health concerns, consult your healthcare provider before participating.

    What should I wear?

    Whatever is comfortable and allows some movement. Business casual works fine — no need to change into workout clothes.

    What do I take away from the event?

    Practical techniques for movement and breathing you can use at your desk and throughout the day, plus approved digital resources shared after the event.

    The short version

    • • We bring wellness experts to your workplace event.
    • • Everyone participates at their own level — seated, standing, or observing.
    • • It's educational, not medical. No diagnosis, no therapy, no prescriptions.
    • • You provide the space and people. We coordinate the rest.

    In exploration

    We're exploring repeat-visit formats, multi-day programs, and an employee benefits directory so individuals can continue working with facilitators after corporate events. These are not available yet — ask during planning if you're interested.
