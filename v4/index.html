The Experience

Step inside a Workplace Wellness event

Explore each chapter of the experience — from arrival to closing conversation.

Arrive and settle in

Arrive to a prepared space. Choose your level of participation. No pressure, no performance. The facilitators greet your team and explain how the session works — everyone participates at their own comfort level.

Facilitators: Sean & Brandon · Duration: ~20 minutes

Your participation choices

Before anything begins, every participant chooses how they want to engage. Follow along fully, adapt activities to your comfort, or simply observe and absorb. Every choice is equally valued.

No equipment needed · All fitness levels welcome

Guided movement with Sean

Sean guides gentle, accessible movement. Stand, sit, or simply observe — every choice is supported. The focus is on awareness and ease, not athletic performance.

Facilitator: Sean · Duration: ~40 minutes

What participants experience

Simple posture resets, gentle stretching sequences, and movement patterns designed for office workers. Everything can be done in business-casual attire with no special equipment.

Seated options available · Adaptable to any space

Guided breathing with Brandon

Brandon leads guided breathing techniques. Follow along, adapt to your comfort, or just rest. These are practical tools your team can use long after the event.

Facilitator: Brandon · Duration: ~40 minutes

Techniques your team will learn

Focused breathing patterns for stress management, energy regulation, and mental clarity. Brandon creates a calm, unhurried space where everyone can find their own rhythm.

Seated or standing · Eyes open or closed

Understanding the why

Learn why movement and breathing matter for workplace wellbeing. Practical takeaways, not theory. Your team hears the science in plain language and gets tools they can use at their desks.

Evidence-based content · Practical application

Takeaways that stick

Every participant leaves with simple techniques they can practice independently — no apps, no subscriptions, no equipment. Just knowledge they own.

Optional post-event resource sharing available

Meaningful group conversation

Share reflections with your group. Conversations that build understanding across teams. This isn't forced sharing — it's a facilitated space for genuine connection.

Facilitated discussion · Duration: ~30 minutes

Why connection matters

Teams that share an experience together work differently afterwards. The conversation chapter turns individual experiences into shared understanding.

Small group or full team format

Closing and reflection

Leave with practical tools, renewed energy, and a shared experience your team will reference. The facilitators close the session with intention and clarity.

Duration: ~15 minutes

What happens next

Your organizer receives a summary of the session. Optional approved takeaway resources can be shared with participants. The experience continues beyond the room.

Post-event resources · Follow-up options available
