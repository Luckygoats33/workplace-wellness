Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Support Wellness Global ("SWG," "we," "us") handles information collected through the Workplace Wellness website ("Site").

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name, company name, and work email address
  • Event details you provide, such as event type, estimated attendees, preferred dates, location, and additional notes

We collect this information solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential event planning.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide event proposals
  • To communicate with you about Workplace Wellness services
  • To improve our programs and website

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and local storage

This Site uses browser local storage to remember your color-scheme preference across visits. This data stays in your browser and is never transmitted to any server. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics pixels.

Third-party services

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Please refer to GitHub's privacy statement for information on their data practices. We load fonts and animation libraries from trusted CDN providers (cdnjs.cloudflare.com).

Data retention

Inquiry information is retained only as long as necessary to respond to your request and for reasonable follow-up. You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Your rights

You may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for future communications at any time

Children's privacy

This Site is intended for business professionals and is not directed at individuals under the age of 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
