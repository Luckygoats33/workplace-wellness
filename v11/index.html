The experience

Less sitting. More taking part.

Your people aren't an audience to be talked at. Facilitators guide the room through short, practical segments, and everyone gets to try things for themselves.

01/ 05
  1. 01

    Guided participation

    Facilitators lead the room step by step, so nobody has to guess what to do next or perform for colleagues.

  2. 02

    Movement

    Simple, accessible movement that works in event clothing and ordinary meeting spaces. Most of it can be done standing or seated.

  3. 03

    Breathing

    Easy-to-follow breathing practices, which simply means paying attention to how you breathe and trying different paces and patterns.

  4. 04

    Questions

    Time for people to ask what they're genuinely curious about, answered in plain language.

  5. 05

    Discussion

    Moments to compare notes with colleagues and leave with ideas people can use in an ordinary workday.

The program

A half-day for the whole room.

The core experience pairs two facilitators with complementary focuses: nervous system, movement and practical education, then breathing and related practices. A full-day version gives more room to go deeper.

Core program

Half-day

Two facilitated sessions, a reset in between and a closing conversation. Fits neatly into a morning or afternoon of an event or offsite.

Extended

Full-day

The same foundation with more time for practice, questions and group discussion. Exact format is shaped with you and the facilitators.

Who it suits

  • HR, people and culture teams planning an employee event
  • Event planners building a retreat or offsite agenda
  • Leadership looking for a shared, hands-on session
  • Company gatherings and annual meetings that want a break from back-to-back presentations

Questions

Questions, answered.

For event planners & buyers

Can the program be customized for our event?

Yes, within reason. We shape the format, timing and emphasis around your audience and agenda, and agree the final details with the facilitators before anything is confirmed.

What do we need to provide at the venue?

Typically a room where people can stand and move a little, chairs that can be moved, and basic audio-visual support. We confirm space, accessibility and audio-visual needs together during planning.

Are the optional additions required?

No. The core program stands alone. Additions like Posture Guy Mike or other practitioners are only there if you want them and your event has room for them.

Can employees book sessions individually?

That's still being decided. For now the focus is facilitated experiences booked by organizations for their events. If individual sessions matter to you, mention it in your inquiry.

Is this medical advice or treatment?

No. This is general wellness education and guided participation. It isn't medical care, therapy or a treatment program, and we don't promise specific health or business results.

For attendees

Do I need any prior experience?

None at all. Everything is explained step by step in plain language, and there's nothing to prepare beforehand.

What if I'm not comfortable joining in?

That's completely fine. You can join in, adapt, observe or step out at any point, and you never have to explain why.

Can activities be adapted for me?

Yes. Facilitators offer seated, smaller or modified versions, and you can always do less than what's shown. If you have a medical condition or any concerns, follow the advice of your own healthcare professional.

What should I wear?

Whatever you'd normally wear to the event. Movement is designed to work in everyday work clothes.

Will I be asked to share health information?

No. You don't need to share any health information to take part, and you're never asked to explain how you choose to participate.

The short version

  • General wellness education. Not medical care, therapy or treatment.
  • Always optional. Join in, adapt, observe or step out.
  • No health data. Nobody needs to share health information to take part.
  • No promised outcomes. Just practical ideas people can try.
